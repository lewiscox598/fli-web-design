Case study

Pigging suitability evaluation

Client
Energy technology company
Region
Offshore India
Services
  • ILI services

We were asked to review the design of a newly installed system offshore India, which included a flexible riser and a multi-diameter subsea carbon steel pipework.

Considering the materials and diameter changes, we were also asked to review the pigging suitability associated with three proposed tools:

  1. Gauge plate inspection
  2. Ultrasonic metal loss inspection
  3. Ultrasonic crack detection

01

Project stats

Tools reviewed
3
Water depth
415 m
System length
24 km

02

The approach

We worked with each proposed pigging vendor to investigate the following:

  • Proposed Design
  • Proposed materials
  • Tool validation
  • Tool performance specification
  • Experience

A summary report was written reviewing each solution, identifying areas of concern and making recommendations to support further validation.

03

Results

We provided the client with an evaluation of the solution for the entire system and identified any areas of concern where further work may be required.

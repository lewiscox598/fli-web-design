Additional engineering services

Alongside our specialist pipeline integrity department, our engineering team offer expert support across industries such as oil and gas, renewables, water and mining. We develop project solutions throughout the infrastructure life cycle, ranging from upfront planning and feasibility studies, engineering, construction through operation and maintenance.
